How the optimiser works

Three comparative scenarios: Full coverage (every applicable intervention), Fixed budget (cheapest first) and Optimised — a greedy knapsack that repeatedly selects the intervention with the highest tCO₂e reduced per USD given what remains to abate.

No double-counting: each selected intervention draws down the remaining baseline of its emission category, so a second refrigerant swap competes on residual refrigerant emissions, not the original total. Summed reductions can never exceed the baseline.

End-use share caps: device-level interventions are bounded by the plausible share of a category their end-use represents (US EIA CBECS 2018 / CIBSE Guide F splits, cooling raised for hot climates):

Known limitations — read before citing results
  1. Factor-based accounting carries ±15–30% uncertainty. Verdex uses published average factors, not metered measurements. Treat results as decision-support estimates, not compliance-grade inventories.
  2. Intervention reduction percentages are catalogue defaults drawn from two LMIC hospital case studies (HIGH Horizons D3.7). Your site will differ — enter site-specific values wherever you have them; site data always overrides defaults.
  3. End-use share caps are assumptions, approximated from commercial-building end-use surveys. They bound over-claiming but are not measured for your building.
  4. Spend-based procurement factors are coarse. The ~0.5 kg CO₂e/USD EEIO factor averages across supply chains; supplier-specific data is always better where available.
  5. Costs are in USD using catalogue-era prices; local procurement will differ.
Community validation — status and roadmap

The intervention catalogue was built from facility case studies and published factors — it has not yet been validated by a broad community of facility managers and local engineers, and we say so openly. What exists today, and what is planned:

  • Today: every default (cost, savings, reduction %) is overridable per site, so local knowledge always wins over the catalogue.
  • Today: this page publishes every factor and assumption for critique — if a number looks wrong for your context, it can be challenged and corrected.
  • Planned: a structured expert-review round (Delphi-style) of the intervention library with facility engineers and sustainability officers across partner countries.
  • Planned: feed anonymised, site-reported actuals (measured savings and costs) back into the catalogue so defaults converge on real-world performance.
