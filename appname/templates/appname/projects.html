Funders ask for the footprint of an award, not an organisation. Wellcome's environmental sustainability funding policy makes staff time to assess and report on an award's emissions an eligible cost, and expects an auditable record where staff work across more than one award. Each project here carries its own footprint, its own sites, and the basis used to apportion shared emissions.
